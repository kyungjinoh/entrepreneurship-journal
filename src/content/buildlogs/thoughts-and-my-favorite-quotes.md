## Thoughts

Just random thoughts flowing through my head.

1. You can't just do it for position. Do it because you love it.

2. Live in this moment.

3. Intellectual curiosity, passion, and finding the right environment to thrive are three crucial traits for achieving success in life. It's an area I know I have to improve on.

4. Climbing higher means nothing if you forget why you started. Sometimes the bravest success is letting go and becoming who you truly are.

5. Technology progresses over time. Technology drives society forward. Harmful technology reverses that progress. It has the power to damage or destroy society. Instead of moving us forward, harmful technology can push humanity back to where it all began.

6. Life exists in defiance of the universe’s tendency toward disorder. It is not something that arises by accident. To live a fulfilling life, then, is to create what is unlikely to occur by chance. This means choosing actions that add meaning. It means sharing friendships, making love, exercising, inventing, and exploring, rather than remaining passive, lying in bed, and scrolling through YouTube Shorts.

5. "The purpose of life is to discover your gift. The work of life is to develop it. The meaning of life is to give your gift away." — David Viscott

6. "Let go of those who bring you down and surround yourself with those who bring out the best in you." — Unknown

7. "One day, your life will flash before your eyes. Make sure it's worth watching." — Gerard Way

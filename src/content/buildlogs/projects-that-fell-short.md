I've failed at many projects or haven't launched them. I just want to show every single failure here. For some ideas, I spent 6 months just ideating. For another, I threw it away after thinking about it for 11 minutes. I will record them all.

Please understand that I am filling this out a while after some projects ended, so hope you understand.

## Moodie: AI Therapist

Moodie was an AI therapist that talks like a human and looks like a human. I decided to create this after realizing that many people are suffering with mental health issues, while not all of them have money to afford mental health services.

I found and brought in two people from LinkedIn and brought them into a Discord chat.

![](/images/fell-short-1.png)

I even designed the logo for it and detailed the ideas.

![](/images/fell-short-2.png)

![](/images/fell-short-3.png)

The next step was trying to find an AI model that we can use. But here comes a problem. All the AI voice models were super expensive. I contacted Hume AI, which charged like $10 per 10 minutes of therapy. At the same time, I realized there were a lot of alternative products that users could use.

We ended up shutting this down. But at least I learned that I have to look at the entire market first before deciding whether I should continue a project.

Next mistake was spending too much time trying to get advice from different people.

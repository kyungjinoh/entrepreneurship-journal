## How did I start?

In the summer of 2025, I attended the LaunchX Entrepreneurship Program, a summer program where I met a lot of talented kids and fellow founders by the end of the program. During the program, I got close to Albert, Chase, and Kymi, and we built Step Up during the batch. What really drew me into it was the social mission to connect highschoolers with nonprofits and internships. Highschoolers are the most underused resources in the job market. And I believed if all the highschoolers volunteered and contributed to the world for social impact, I could make the world significantly better. After the program, I stayed on as a co-founder, developer, and outreach lead.

## Partnering with Organizations

The first thing I thought I should work on was onboarding as many company to our website as possible and make sure that we have a lot of company base before launching this to our customer, students. For this reason, the first thing I did was making partnership with companies. The listed below was all the companies we partnered up up to this point but it wasn't enough.

## Building Discord Community

Another thing that I did was bringing users into our discord group. There are two ways. First is directly asking our users or friend and second is bringing users from schoolclicker. I decided to combine discord chat for step up with schoolclicker, since they share same customer base and also help students who are using schoolclicker to find an internship.

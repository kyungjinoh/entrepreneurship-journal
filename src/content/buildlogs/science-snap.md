## Why did I start this?

This started in my science class (I believe it was chemistry class). AP Chemistry class is known as one of the hardest classes to take in our school. What most of the students say, getting out of the AP Chem class, is "Omg!!! This class is so damn hard!" "Oh! I'm going to community college" "I'm lwk dropping out". I believe science should be, first, easy. Science is a study of explaining intuitive using number; it is not twisting intuitive fact and making it harder. Once students see science as a study that applies in the intuitive real world, science will be accessible and you won't need AP Chem teacher to learn advanced chemistry, even making it accessible for underserved students.

## Growing the platform

Next, I optimized SEO. I installed NitroPack, an app that speeds up the website speed. I tracked top Science keyword in "Google Trends" and tried to use as many of those keywords in my website. I set up google search console to track visitors and number of searches. I did the same thing for all the pages in the website. I did the same thing for all the images on putting alt text and meta text. After all these were finished, I moved on to recruiting writers.

## Building the team

This was probably the hardest part of everything, since I didn't have any networks. Here were my strategy. In each schools, I gather school leaders who will establish their school chapters in their own school so that they will recruit writers in their own school.

The problem now was recruiting those leaders. One thing was to post the fact that I am recruiting people to write blogs in many communities. I posted in reddit, discord server (which I later realized that spamming was prohibited), and even mom's community who can refer to their child who is good at writing. By the end of the day, I could recruit about 88 writers who will help write it and form a leadership structure.

![](/images/image79.png)

(all the writers in the wordpress)

Now, after structuring leadership position and organization, now my step was to clearly articulate my vision to my writers and educate them. I had a meeting with all the chapter leaders so that they know what to do, gave them wordpress authority and id, and make sure that our mission is to make science easy and accessible, not to make it extra complicated. I decided to make guideline writers should follow so that they both manage SEO and focus on making science easy.

## Scaling the Organization

After setting up this structure, aiming and articulating clearly to chapter leaders every month that we should write article every 1-2 months, our platform quickly grew. Now, there is over 130 posts in our website.

![](/images/image102.png)

I set up Instagram page. I post our post on reddit. I sent to my friends. I post on discord. I asked my friends to share to their friend to share to their friend. I got the feedback. Iterated. Opened subscription model (which was free).

Now, everything was consistency. Result was profounding. I got 1.5k+ subscribers in our mailing list. Got 1M impression (maybe more since tracking data before 2025 March was erased).
